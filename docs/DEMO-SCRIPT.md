## Part 2: 3-Minute Live Reviewer Fast Path

For judges and reviewers evaluating the live build:

1. **Step 1 (0:00 – 0:30): Start the Mission**
   - Click **Start Mission** on the title screen.
   - Watch the 28-second in-engine Three.js cinematic establishing the 7 key outpost stations, or press **Escape / Enter** to skip to the briefing.
   - Enter your callsign (default: *Commander Nova*) and rover name (default: *Rusty*). Click **Begin Expedition**.

2. **Step 2 (0:30 – 1:15): Experience Mars Physics & Chores**
   - Click anywhere to lock mouse cursor for smooth camera control.
   - Use **WASD** to walk to the yellow beacon ($0.863\text{ m/s}$ calibrated walking speed).
   - Press **Space** to experience Mars suited gravity ($3.73\text{ m/s}^2$) and collect the 3 glowing gravity crystals.
   - Read the Mars Gravity science card.

3. **Step 3 (1:15 – 2:00): Mission 1 (Whisper in Tank 2)**
   - Walk to Oxygen Tank 2. Hear the procedural tick-hiss audio.
   - Press **E** to inspect the pipes.
   - Answer the valve quiz (*"How can a valve help with a leak?"*).
   - Make your decision: **Choice 1: Close the damaged valve**.
   - Earn the **Oxygen Guardian** badge.

4. **Step 4 (2:00 – 3:00): Reviewer Shortcut to Mission 5**
   - Press **Escape** to open the Pause Menu.
   - Under **Skip Ahead (For Reviewers)**, click **Jump to Mission 5 (Sol 88)**.
   - Experience the Sol 88 space weather storm alert, inspect the radiation shelter water-jacket walls, and coordinate crew safety.
   - Press **F** to inspect the real-time Mars Science Lens sensor telemetry.
